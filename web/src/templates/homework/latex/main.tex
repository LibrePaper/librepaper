\problem{1}{10}

Solve the following equation:
\[
  x^2 + 3x + 2 = 0
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the roots of this quadratic equation.
\vspace{2cm}
\item[(b)] Verify your solutions by substituting back into the original equation.
\vspace{2cm}
\end{enumerate}

\problem{2}{8}

Calculate the derivative of the function:
\[
  f(x) = 3x^3 - 2x^2 + x - 5
\]
Show your work step by step.
\vspace{3cm}

\problem{3}{7}

Evaluate the definite integral:
\[
  \int_0^2 (2x + 1) \, dx
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Set up the integral.
\vspace{1cm}
\item[(b)] Compute the antiderivative.
\vspace{1cm}
\item[(c)] Evaluate using the fundamental theorem of calculus.
\vspace{2cm}
\end{enumerate}

\problem{4}{5}

\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] All prime numbers are odd.
\item[(b)] The sum of any two even numbers is even.
\item[(c)] Every positive integer is a prime number.
\item[(d)] Zero is a positive number.
\end{enumerate}
