This chapter reports the simulation results. The estimates in \cref{tab:book-results} come from 500 replications, and each row follows the design in \cref{chap:methods}.

Regression adjustment has the smallest standard error. The bootstrap intervals cover close to the nominal rate, which supports the check described in \cref{eq:bootstrap-se}.

\section{Interpretation}\label{sec:interpretation}

The three estimators agree on the size of the effect. The choice between them comes down to precision and the cost of the extra modeling step, as \citet{rubin1974} anticipated for randomized designs. Chapter~\ref{chap:introduction} frames why these trade-offs matter for the questions the book opens with.
