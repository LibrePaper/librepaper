---
title: "What is LibrePaper"
---

LibrePaper is a free and open source platform for collaborative writing. Write
with colleagues, collect comments on a published document, or connect an LLM
agent to comment and edit.

![The LibrePaper editor: LaTeX source on the left, the compiled paper with its figure, equation and table on the right.](../images/editor-latex.png)

## Features

- **Live preview**: Markdown, HTML, Typst and LaTeX
- **Computational notebooks**: Quarto and Calepin
- **Authoring**: templates, tools, and multi-file projects
- **Bibliographies**: BibTeX and Zotero
- **Track changes**: accept or reject each edit
- **Simultaneous editors**: named carets and personal undo
- **Annotate**: highlights, suggested edits and threaded comments, live for everyone
- **Share**: readers, commenters or editors, signed in with GitHub or Google
- **Chat**: with collaborators and AI agents
- **History**: granular and infinite
- **Bring your own agent**: no new subscription
- **Agent chat**: ask for changes in the sidebar
- **Pre-specified tasks**: pick one with a passage selected
- **Agent permissions**: reader, commenter, or editor
- **Private**: LibrePaper doesn't need your keys or tokens
- **MCP**: any commercial model or local weights
- **Everything is free**: no gated features
- **Open source**: server, reader and renderers
- **Public host**: 50 MB of storage per user and 30 uploads per hour at librepaper.org
- **Self-host**: one static binary and a Postgres database, on a laptop or a small server
- **Export**: projects and comments as ordinary files, from the CLI
- **Local backups**: every project in an account, as ZIP files, with the companion
